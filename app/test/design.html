Tactics Trainer — Design System

The idea

A fixed instrument panel, not a page. Three rows that never move: readouts on top, board in the middle sized to whatever is left over, and the two answer buttons pinned in the thumb zone. Nothing scrolls during play, so scrollToBottom() has nothing left to do.

  • Fixed shell. 100dvh + env(safe-area-inset-*), overflow:hidden, overscroll-behavior:none. The board sizes itself from container-query units, so it shrinks to fit rather than pushing the buttons off-screen.
  • Thumb-first. Answer buttons are 74 pt tall (80 on tall phones), full half-width each, with a 16 px dead gutter between them. Secondary controls are 40 pt, live in the top corners outside the thumb arc, and anything destructive is gated behind a confirm sheet.
  • Never colour-only. Safe/Unsafe differ in hue and lightness and each carries an icon plus a word. Feedback adds a ring and motion. The history list adds a rail, an icon and a verdict label.
  • No numeric jitter. Every live number uses tabular figures, and the countdown digit has a fixed advance width.
  • Themes. Light and dark, following the OS by default; data-theme on <html> overrides in both directions. Board palettes are tuned per theme — warm wood in light, cool slate in dark, because a bright wood board glares at night.

Board tokens

The 11 properties in TOKENS[] from board-interface.js. Both renderers read these at runtime, so the names are a contract. The checkerboard below is drawn from --board-light / --board-dark by .board-slot; the coloured squares are the highlight kinds.

The light theme’s light square sits at L* 81.7, deliberately close to the dark theme’s L* 82.2. A white piece is L* 98.3, so anything lighter than about L* 83 leaves too little albedo between piece and square for the 3D renderer to separate them once a flat square catches more light than a curved piece body. Both themes now give the renderer the same problem to solve.
--board-coord-on-light / --board-coord-on-dark are additions beyond TOKENS[]: reusing the opposite square’s fill as label ink only reaches 2.2:1 against its own square, which is not legible at 11px.
--piece-black is an albedo, not a rendered colour: at L* 23 it is ebonized boxwood, not near-black. A curved piece body returns only about a quarter of its albedo luminance in the 3D rig, so a near-black albedo lands near rendered L* 3 — where the entire form spans under 3 L* and reads as a flat silhouette. Real tournament dark sets are L* 15-25 and warm, so this is the realistic value as well as the legible one. The 2D board is unaffected: it ships Cburnett artwork with baked-in fills.

Motion

--dur-1 90ms press · --dur-2 160ms state · --dur-3 240ms enter/exit · --dur-4 380ms sheets · --dur-5 700ms verdict. Easings: --ease-out, --ease-in, --ease-in-out, --ease-spring.

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the durations collapse to 1 ms at the token level, and shakes, pulses, spinners and shimmers are switched off outright. Nothing in the app depends on movement to be understood.
